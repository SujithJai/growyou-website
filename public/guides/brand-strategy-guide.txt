GroYou Brand Strategy Guide

1. Clarify who you serve and what problem you solve best.
2. Define the premium promise and evidence behind it.
3. Build a positioning statement your team can repeat clearly.
4. Create offer hierarchy across flagship, core and entry products.
5. Align visual identity with category expectations and ambition.
6. Establish tone of voice for sales, ads, website and social.
7. Translate brand strategy into landing pages and campaign hooks.
8. Build a content system around authority, proof and differentiation.
9. Make trust assets visible across the funnel.
10. Ensure every channel expresses the same strategic story.
