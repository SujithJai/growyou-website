GroYou Google Ads Checklist

1. Separate branded, non-branded and competitor intent.
2. Confirm accurate conversion tracking in GA4 + GTM.
3. Match offers and keywords to dedicated landing pages.
4. Review search terms weekly and add negatives aggressively.
5. Use audience overlays to improve intent signals.
6. Test multiple headline and description angles.
7. Segment Performance Max around products, margins or geographies.
8. Map lead quality back into campaign optimisation.
9. Align budgets to high-intent ad groups first.
10. Report on qualified pipeline and revenue, not only leads.
