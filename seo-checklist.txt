GroYou SEO Checklist

1. Validate indexing and crawlability.
2. Fix broken internal links and redirect chains.
3. Improve Core Web Vitals and page speed.
4. Map every service to dedicated search-intent pages.
5. Build location pages for local demand capture.
6. Strengthen title tags, meta descriptions and H1 structure.
7. Add FAQ, service and organization schema.
8. Improve on-page conversion paths and CTA visibility.
9. Build topical authority clusters around commercial keywords.
10. Track enquiries, calls and CRM stages back to organic traffic.
